# Newbie Shield and War Frenzy

## Newbie shield

Every new Keeper's castle starts under a **newbie shield**. A shielded castle **cannot be attacked or scouted** by other players.

The shield lasts **until your Castle reaches level 10** or for **14 days** after you create your hero — whichever comes first.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Your own aggression ends your shield for ever.** The moment you attack or scout another player, your shield falls — even if you are still below Castle level 10.
{% endhint %}

The shield protects your **castle**. Your armies out on the map — gathering or holding an asteroid — are not covered by it.

On the map, a shielded castle is shown with its shield, so you can see at a glance who can be attacked.

## War Frenzy

Any act of aggression puts your Keeper into **War Frenzy** for **15 minutes**:

* besieging another player's castle;
* attacking an asteroid held by another player's army;
* scouting another player's castle, or an asteroid their army holds.

During War Frenzy **you cannot teleport your castle** and **you cannot put on a shield**. Every new attack or scout starts the 15 minutes again.

The time left is shown next to the teleport button.

{% hint style="info" %}
War Frenzy is there so that an attacker cannot strike and then jump away. If you start a fight, you stay where you are for a while.
{% endhint %}
