# Create Your Hero

Creating a hero takes two short steps: **the hero** and **the password**.

## The hero

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Name

3 to 16 characters: letters, digits, `_` and `-`. This is how others see you in chat, in the Circle of War and on the map. Some names of the old Keepers are reserved.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Sex and portrait

Pick the look of your hero. It changes nothing in battle.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Faction — for ever

**Aurelion** (north, blue) or **Umbrael** (south, red). The faction decides on whose half of the sphere your castle stands and colours your interface. See [The Two Factions](../world/the-two-factions.md).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Class

* **Warrior** — physical damage, fights with **Rage**.
* **Mage** — magic damage, fights with **Mana**.

At level 5 you choose one of four subclasses, at level 15 an element. See [Classes and Subclasses](../hero/classes-and-subclasses.md).
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## The password

Choose a strong password — the meter under the field shows how strong it is. Your password is never shown to anybody, and changing it later ends every other session of your account.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The faction, the class, and later the subclass, the element and the profession are **chosen once**. Take a moment before you confirm.
{% endhint %}

## What comes next

After you create the hero you arrive at **your castle** and meet **Vedar Highnoon**, mentor of the Circle of War. Continue with [Your First Hour](your-first-hour.md).
