# Your First Hour

_"I am Vedar Highnoon, mentor of the Circle of War. This castle is yours now, and the hero is your hand in battle. Let me show you where to start: it takes a couple of minutes."_

## Vedar's three first steps

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Raise the castle to level 3

The first builds are fast — under a minute each. Open the castle (key **H**), choose the Castle and press **Upgrade**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open the hero and spend the free points

There is no battle without it. Press **C**, put your free points into characteristics, see what changes and press **Save**. Then spend your combat skill points the same way.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Press "To battle" and defeat the Dummy

On the map, press **To battle**, then **Train with the Dummy**. The Dummy is a fighter of your level that strikes fair and takes no offence. Beating it is how a young hero gains levels.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## The Keeper's Path

After Vedar's steps the **Keeper's Path** takes over. Its current goal is always at the top of the screen. **Click it** — the game takes you exactly where the goal is done. Every step pays **Seals** and **speed-ups**.

The Path walks you through everything the game has:

* raising the Castle and building the first barracks — the first squad is a gift;
* choosing your subclass at level 5;
* clearing the **Amber Grotto**, your first dungeon;
* fighting a 5 on 5 battle in the Circle of War;
* sending gatherers to an asteroid and defeating a monster on the map;
* buying an item in Tala's shop;
* joining a legion or founding your own.

When the Path is done, **daily tasks** and **achievements** lead on. See [Goals and Rewards](../reference/goals-and-rewards.md).

## Useful keys

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **H** | Into your castle and back to the map |
| **C** | Hero page: characteristics, gear, skills |
| **I** | Shared inventory: Seals, speed-ups, items |
| **R** | Fly the camera to your castle |
| **M** | The big map |
| **F3** or **/** | Search for players and places |
| **Space** | Confirm the main action in many windows |

{% hint style="success" %}
**You are protected while you grow.** A new Keeper's castle has a newbie shield. See [Newbie Shield and War Frenzy](newbie-shield-and-war-frenzy.md).
{% endhint %}
