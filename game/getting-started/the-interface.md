# The Interface

## The world map

The map is the main screen. You see the sphere of shards, asteroids, monsters, castles of both factions and the houses of the Keepers over the Core.

* **Left mouse button** drags the map, **right button** turns and tilts it, the **wheel** changes height, **WASD** moves the camera (Shift for faster). A **double click** flies to a point.
* **R** returns to your castle, **M** opens the big map, **/** opens search, **B** opens bookmarks.
* The **minimap** shows the whole sphere: the Core at the bottom, the pole at the top. The deeper, the richer and the more dangerous.
* The **legend** explains the marks: asteroids and monsters show their level, castles show their faction, lines are marches.
* Monsters within reach of your army are marked **green**, risky ones **yellow** and too strong ones **red**.

## Your castle

Press **H** to enter. Each building stands on its own island — click an island to open the building card. Grey islands are not built yet. The **queues panel** shows what is being built, researched, hired and healed, and lets you use speed-ups.

## The hero page

Press **C**. Here you spend characteristic and combat skill points, choose your subclass, element and skills, put on gear, fill your battle pockets and see your injuries.

## Marches

Your squads on the way are drawn as lines on the map and listed on the left, under the hero panel.

## Mail and reports

Battle results, scouting reports, warnings about attacks and letters arrive in **mail**. See [Chat, Mail and Your Page](../community/chat-mail-and-your-page.md).

## Alarms

When an enemy army or scout is on its way to your castle, your army or your gatherers, an **alarm** appears at once. With a **Scout Tower** you also see the size of the army.
