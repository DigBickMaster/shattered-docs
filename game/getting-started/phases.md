# Phases

Phase 1 tests Shattered Worlds on its own. Phase 2 brings Decision Arena and Amber into it. Then comes the release.

## How the test phases run

* **Each phase starts on a brand-new world.** Nothing carries over from one phase to the next.
* **Servers open one at a time.** When a server is full, the next one opens.
* **Each server runs for a set time cycle.** When the team has collected enough data, the next phase is announced.
* **Lots of speed-ups.** During Phase 1 and Phase 2 the team hands out many speed-ups and other boosts to everyone, so that testing goes fast.
* **Your own AI agents are welcome.** In both test phases you may connect your own AI agent and let it play as you like. See [AI Agents](../community/ai-agents.md).
* **Found a hole? Report it — do not use it.** Every report gets a number. A confirmed hole is rewarded with speed-ups or cosmetics, and your name goes into the public changelog.

## Phase 1 — Shattered Worlds on its own

* Phase 1 has the hero RPG, the castle, the army, the map and legions — everything in this guide that is not marked *Coming in Phase 2* or *Coming at release*.
* **The goal is data:** balance, levelling, load and what the game is still missing. Every Keeper who plays helps.

## The invitation to Phase 2

Open **Settings**, add your **e-mail** and tick **Invite me to Phase 2**. Other players never see your e-mail. See [Settings and Account](../community/settings-and-account.md).

When Phase 2 is announced, every Keeper with an e-mail and the tick gets an **e-mail with a link**. Register through that link and you receive the **Pathfinder** badge — the badge of a Phase 1 participant — right away.

## Phase 2 — Shattered Worlds and Decision Arena together

* The servers relaunch on a **new world, with all updates**. A new test cycle starts from the beginning.
* Shattered Worlds and **Decision Arena** are joined into one game. Decision Arena, the card duel, opens inside the game — free cards for everyone. See [Decision Arena](../decision-arena/decision-arena.md).
* **Amber** opens at **hero level 15**, together with your wallet. See [Amber](../amber/amber.md).

## Release

* **The release castle.** Keepers who take part in Phase 2 and continue to the release receive a **unique castle**. It is an NFT that is always shown as a **unique castle skin**.
  * It is a **trophy**: it **cannot be sold or transferred**.
  * It stays **for ever on the account that received it**.
  * It changes how your castle looks — nothing else.
* **Subscriptions** arrive only at release. See [Subscriptions](../economy/subscriptions.md).
* The **AI connection** from the shop arrives at release. See [AI Agents](../community/ai-agents.md).
