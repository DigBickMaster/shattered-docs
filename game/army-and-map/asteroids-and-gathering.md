# Asteroids and Gathering

**Asteroids** are pieces of the old world drifting between the shards. They hold **iron**, **energy** and **crystal** — everything your castle is built with.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Gathering opens at Castle level 3.**
{% endhint %}

## Sending gatherers

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Find an asteroid

Start with one near your castle. Its level is shown on the map; the deeper towards the Core, the higher the level and the richer the asteroid.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Click it and press Gather

Choose how many gatherers to send. The window shows the way, the cargo and the time.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Wait for the cargo

The gatherers mine while they stay on the asteroid. The resources arrive at the castle **when the gatherers are home**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Asteroid levels

* Asteroids go from **level 1 to level 6**.
* A higher asteroid holds more and takes longer to mine fully: a level 1 asteroid is mined out in about two hours, a level 6 one in about five.
* Gathering on higher-level asteroids needs the University science **Deep Gathering**.
* A mined-out asteroid comes back after a while.

## Asteroids held by others

* If another player's **army holds an asteroid**, your gatherers cannot go there. Take it with an army first.
* An army can **hold an asteroid** for you as a bridgehead — it stays there until you call it home.
* Battles on asteroids happen across the faction line. The fallen of both sides go to their Infirmaries; beyond the beds they die.

See [Sieges and Battles for Asteroids](sieges-and-battles-for-asteroids.md).

## Legion territory

Asteroids inside a legion's territory give the legion's members **more** when they gather there. See [The Legion Castle and Territory](../legions/legion-castle-and-territory.md).

## Your gatherers are not shielded

The newbie shield protects your castle only. Gatherers out on an asteroid can be attacked — an **alarm** warns you when an army is on its way to them.
