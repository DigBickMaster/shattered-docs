# The Map

The world map shows the whole **sphere of shards**: your castle and the castles of others, asteroids, monsters and the houses of the Keepers over the Core.

## Up and down the sphere

The minimap shows the sphere from the side: **the Core at the bottom, the pole at the top**. The deeper you go towards the Core, **the richer the asteroids and the more dangerous the monsters**.

## The Veil

The **Veil** splits the sphere into the northern half (Aurelion) and the southern half (Umbrael).

* **No army marches through the Veil.**
* What is behind the Veil cannot be reached, attacked or scouted from your side.
* An army coming from beyond the Veil is not shown on your map until it is through.

## Coordinates and search

* Every place has **coordinates**. Reports of scouting and sieges show them, so you can find the place again.
* **Search** (**F3** or **/**): find a player and their castle, an asteroid or a monster. **Show** flies the camera there and opens the card.
* **Bookmarks** (**B**) keep places you want to return to.

## What you see on the map

| Mark | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Asteroid with a number | An asteroid; the number is its level |
| Monster with a number | A monster; the number is its level |
| Blue / red castles | Aurelion / Umbrael castles |
| A castle with a shield | A newbie-shielded castle — cannot be attacked or scouted |
| A line | A march: for resources, or an attack |
| Green / yellow / red monster | Within reach / risky / too strong for your army |

## Marches

A **march** is a squad on its way: gatherers going to an asteroid, an army going to a monster, an asteroid or a castle, or a scout. Marches are drawn as lines on the map and listed on the left under the hero panel. You can **recall** a march home.

## The castle teleport

A **teleport** moves your castle to an asteroid: open the asteroid's window and press **Teleport**.

* You get a teleport, for example, from the **Castle level 10** milestone.
* You **cannot teleport during War Frenzy** — see [Newbie Shield and War Frenzy](../getting-started/newbie-shield-and-war-frenzy.md).
