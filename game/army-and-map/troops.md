# Troops

Your army is hired in three barracks. Each kind of troop beats one kind and loses to another — a circle with no single best choice.

| Troop | Barracks | Fights as | Strong against | Weak against |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Shard Wardens** | Shard Warden Barracks | Infantry, a wall of shields — the base of any army | Spark Archers | Harpooners |
| **Harpooners** | Harpooner Barracks | Long harpoons that keep the enemy at a distance | Shard Wardens | Spark Archers |
| **Spark Archers** | Spark Archer Barracks | Arrows with an amber spark that strike from afar | Harpooners | Shard Wardens |

**Gatherers** are not fighters. They mine resources on asteroids and work all the time while they stay there. They are hired one by one in any barracks.

{% hint style="success" %}
The first squad from each barracks is a gift — a castle quest pays it when the barracks is built.
{% endhint %}

## Hiring

* Choose the troop and the amount in the barracks. The troops come out in turn; the whole order has one price and one time.
* While the hire queue is busy, a new order waits — or use a speed-up.
* **Tiers.** The University opens higher tiers of every troop: the higher the tier, the stronger and the dearer.

## The Dock

All your troops and gatherers stand at the **Dock**. Click it to see the whole army: how many fighters and gatherers you have and where they are now. Armies leave from the Dock on marches.

## The wounded and the Infirmary

After a battle your fallen soldiers go to the **Infirmary** — as long as it has free beds.

* **Beyond the beds, the fallen die.** Without an Infirmary, every soldier who falls dies.
* Healing costs a part of the hire price and time — much less than hiring new troops.
* Every level of the Infirmary adds beds and speeds up healing.
* Heal quickly to keep the beds free for the next battle.

{% hint style="warning" %}
When you attack a castle, most of your fallen go to the Infirmary and the rest die. Defenders' fallen go to their own Infirmary.
{% endhint %}
