# Monsters

Monsters roam the map between the shards. Beating them gives **loot** and moves goals and the legion's weekly goal.

| Monster | What to know |
| --- | --- |
| **Driftbeast** | A creature of the drifting asteroids. The first monsters you meet near your castle. |
| **Amberwraith** | A wraith drawn to Amber. |
| **Voidborn Guardian** | Guards the deep places. Falls only to a **group of players**. |

Every monster shows its **level** on the map. The deeper towards the Core, the stronger they are.

## Attacking a monster

1. Click the monster and press **Attack**.
2. Choose the troops. **The forecast and the loot are shown before the army leaves.**
3. Send the army. The battle happens on arrival; the report comes in mail.

{% hint style="success" %}
Monsters within reach of your army are marked **green** on the map, risky ones **yellow**, and too strong ones **red**.
{% endhint %}

* A beaten monster **comes back later**.
* Losses go to your Infirmary while it has beds.
* Your first victory over a monster completes a castle quest.
