# Sieges and Battles for Asteroids

## Sieging a castle

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Scout first

Without a scout report the defenders are hidden and there is no forecast. See [Scouting](scouting.md).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose the troops

The siege window shows the way, the load your army can carry and — after scouting — the battle forecast.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### The battle happens on arrival

The defender gets a **warning at once** when your army sets out. All the troops in the defending castle defend it.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### Loot and losses

* The attacker loots **only what is above the defender's protected stock**, and only what the surviving troops can carry.
* The attacker's fallen: most go to the Infirmary, the rest die.
* The defender's fallen go to their Infirmary; beyond the beds they die.
* The **Wall and Dome** make the castle harder to take.

## Battles for asteroids

* An army sent to an asteroid can **hold it** as a bridgehead until you call it home.
* If another player's army holds the asteroid, your army fights it on arrival.
* The fallen of both sides go to their Infirmaries; beyond the beds they die.
* Battles on the asteroids of **legion beacons and Legion Castles** count for the legion's weekly goal.

## Who can be attacked

* Castles under the **newbie shield** cannot be attacked or scouted.
* Attacking or scouting another player ends your own shield and starts **War Frenzy** for 15 minutes — no teleport and no shield during it.
* Nothing beyond the Veil can be reached.

## Alarms

When an army or a scout is on its way to your castle, your army or your gatherers, an **alarm** appears at once. With a **Scout Tower** you also see how big the army is.
