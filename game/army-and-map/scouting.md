# Scouting

A **scout** flies to a castle or an asteroid held by an army and reports what it saw. Scouting is the way to know what you are going to fight.

## The Scout Tower

The **Scout Tower** opens at Castle level 5. With it:

* you can **look into another castle**;
* you **learn of a raid in advance** — an alarm shows the size of the army coming at you. Without the tower you know that an army is coming, but not how big it is.

## The scout report

The report comes in mail and shows the **defenders**, the **coordinates** and what can be taken.

* Without a scout report, the defenders of a castle stay **hidden** and there is **no battle forecast** — a blind siege is allowed, but you go in without knowing.
* With a report, the siege window **forecasts the battle** against the defenders the scout saw.

## Rules

* You cannot scout a castle under the **newbie shield**.
* Scouting another player is an act of aggression: it ends **your own** newbie shield and starts **War Frenzy**. See [Newbie Shield and War Frenzy](../getting-started/newbie-shield-and-war-frenzy.md).
* Nothing beyond the Veil can be scouted.
* The target gets an **alarm** that a scout is on its way.
