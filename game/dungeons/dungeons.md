# Dungeons

Dungeons are underground runs through rooms full of monsters, chests and traps, with a **boss** at the end. You can go **alone** or with a **party**.

## The dungeons

| Dungeon | Hero levels | Monsters | Boss |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Amber Grotto** | 5–10 | Spark Beetles, Crystal Bats | Grotto Hermit Shellcrab |
| **Dark Mines** | 10–15 | Driller Moles, Cinder Spiders | Ore Broodmother |
| **Forgotten Fortress** | 15–20 | Gargoyle Sentinels, Ash Hounds | Fallen Bastion Warden |

Each dungeon has a **solo** version and a **party** version for **2 to 5** heroes. The party version gives more experience.

Your first run of the Amber Grotto is guided: Vedar walks beside you and gives hints. After that the dungeon becomes a normal one.

## Moving

| Key | Step |
| --- | --- |
| **W** / **S** | Forward / back |
| **A** / **D** | A step sideways |
| **Q** / **E** | Turn the view |

* You see only **two cells ahead**. The minimap opens only where you have been.
* **Monsters roam inside their room** — for each step of yours a monster makes its own.
* In a party, **the leader moves** and the others follow.

## Fights

Step onto a monster's cell and the fight begins: the same zones, skills and pockets as in the Circle of War — see [How a Battle Works](../circle-of-war/how-a-battle-works.md). In a party everyone has their own turn and their own monster.

* Every monster killed gives **experience**.
* A **won fight restores your health**.

## Chests and traps

* Walk up to a **chest** and you take its contents at once: **Seals** and **speed-ups**.
* **Traps** cannot be spotted in advance or disarmed — that is the price of the passage. In harder dungeons they hit harder.

## The boss

The boss has **phases**: as it loses health it changes its moves, may strike an area and call helpers. If you drag the fight out, it goes berserk. Keep your health up and do not miss turns.

## Loot is yours

**What you find stays with you** — even if you leave early or lose. In a party every hero gets their own share.

## Cooldown

After a run — finished, lost or left — the dungeon goes on **cooldown** for several hours. The entrance shows when you can come back.

{% hint style="warning" %}
You cannot enter a dungeon with a **heavy injury**. See [Injuries and Healing](../hero/injuries-and-healing.md).
{% endhint %}

## Parties

* Open the dungeon entrance and **gather your own party** or join an open one — friends see it in the list.
* The leader starts the run when the party is ready.
* While you are in a party, the solo run opens only after you leave the party.
