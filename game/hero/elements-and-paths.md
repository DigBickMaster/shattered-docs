# Elements and Paths

At **level 15** a hero chooses one of four **elements**. The element opens the items and the skills of that element and gives the hero a **path** — a title that tells what kind of fighter they have become.

| Element | Feel |
| --- | --- |
| **Flame** | Fire and onslaught |
| **Frost** | Cold, doom and control |
| **Stone** | Weight, endurance and anchors |
| **Storm** | Lightning, speed and wind |

For the **Guardian** the elements carry their own names: **Core Flame**, **Veil Frost**, **Anchor Stone** and **Shard Storm**.

## The paths

| Subclass | Flame | Frost | Stone | Storm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Guardian** | Bastion Hammer | Doom Warden | Unbreakable | Reflector |
| **Berserker** | Crusher | Executioner | Raging | Blade Whirl |
| **Hunter** | Harpooner | Doom Archer | Trapper | Tracker |
| **Standard Bearer** | Banner of Onslaught | Banner of Fate | Banner of the Wall | Banner of the Wind |
| **Archmage** | Flame Lord | Frost Lord | Stone Lord | Storm Lord |
| **Sorcerer** | Ash Mirage | Ice Mirage | Sand Mirage | Mirage Weaver |
| **Healer** | Hearth Healer | Spring Healer | Root Healer | Spark Healer |
| **Enchanter** | Seal Smith | Ice Keeper | Anchor Architect | Storm Caller |

## What the element changes

* **Gear.** Items of an element suit heroes of that element. The frame of an element item and the name of the path show its colour.
* **Skills.** The skills of the element open with it.
* **Resistances.** Battles also have elemental attack and resistance — gear and socket stones can raise them.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**The element is for ever.** It decides which items and skills suit the hero. Look at the cards carefully before you hold the button.
{% endhint %}

At level 15 the hero also chooses a **profession** — see [Professions](../economy/professions.md).
