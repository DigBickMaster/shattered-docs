# Gear and Inventory

## The hero's equipment

The hero page shows a **doll** with equipment slots: weapon and off-hand, head, torso, hands, belt, legs, feet, and jewellery. Click an item to open it; click an **empty slot** to go straight to Tala's shop on the right tab.

Weapons depend on the class and subclass:

* **Warriors** — swords, axes, maces with a shield; **Hunters** use a bow or a crossbow with a matching quiver; **Standard Bearers** carry a banner.
* **Mages** — staffs, wands, spellbooks and hand seals.

{% hint style="info" %}
A bow or a crossbow needs a **quiver** in the left hand — arrows for a bow, bolts for a crossbow. Take the off-hand item off first.
{% endhint %}

Every set of every subclass is listed in the [Gear Catalog](../gear/README.md).

## Requirements

Every item has a **level** and may require some characteristics. An item whose requirements are no longer met — for example because of an injury — gives nothing until you meet them again.

## Sets

Many items belong to a **set**. When the set is complete, it gives a **set bonus**. Sets open after you choose your subclass.

## Sockets

Gear from **level 15** has a **socket**, gear from **level 20** has two, and the highest gear has three. A **socket stone** made by a Gemcutter goes into a socket and works while the item is worn. Stones can be taken out again.

## Battle pockets

Pockets hold items **for battle** — grenades, stones and scrolls made by professions.

* Your **belt** opens the pockets: a belt from level 5 gives one pocket, a belt from level 15 gives three.
* In a battle you may use **one pocket item per turn**, in addition to your strike.
* Each item has its own number of uses and its own cooldown.

To fill a pocket, pick a "For battle" item in the inventory and press **To pocket**.

## Shared inventory

Press **I** for the Keeper's shared inventory: **Seals**, **speed-ups** and other things that belong to you rather than to the hero's bag.
