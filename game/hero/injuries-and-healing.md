# Injuries and Healing

## Injuries

A hero who falls in a battle **with other players** may get an **injury**. Battles with the Dummy and with monsters do not injure.

| Injury | Passes by itself in | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Light** | 2 hours | Lowers one random characteristic |
| **Medium** | 4 hours | Lowers two random characteristics |
| **Heavy** | 8 hours | Lowers three random characteristics. **No battles and no dungeons** with a heavy injury |

* Falling to a critical strike makes a worse injury more likely.
* You can carry several light and medium injuries, but only one heavy.
* If an injury drops a characteristic below an item's requirement, the item gives nothing until you recover.

Your injuries are listed on the hero page and in the **Professions** window under **Injuries and healing**.

## Healing

Injuries pass by themselves. If you cannot wait, a **Healer** — another player with the Healer profession who is in the game — heals at once for a price in Seals. You cannot heal yourself.

{% hint style="info" %}
The health and resource you lose in a battle come back by themselves over time. A won fight in a dungeon restores your health.
{% endhint %}
