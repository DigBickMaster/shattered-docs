# Classes and Subclasses

Every hero starts as a **Warrior** or a **Mage**. At **level 5** the hero chooses a **subclass** — a role in battle with its own skills and its own gear. The choice is made once and stays with the hero to the end.

## Warrior

Physical damage. A warrior's battle resource is **Rage**: it builds up during the fight and pays for skills.

| Subclass | Role in battle | Signature skills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Guardian** | The wall of the team. Draws enemies onto himself and protects the line. | Shield Bash, Iron Defence, Taunt, Shield Wall, Cover of the Line |
| **Berserker** | Raw damage. The angrier he gets, the harder he hits. | Sweep, Bloodlust, Frenzy, Armour Crush, Whirlwind Strike |
| **Hunter** | A marksman with a bow or a crossbow. Marks targets and pins them down. | Aimed Shot, Snare, Hunter's Mark, Volley, Binding Shot |
| **Standard Bearer** | Leads the team under a banner: boosts allies and frightens enemies. | Under the Banner, Cry of Onslaught, Banner of Fortitude, Banner of Dread |

## Mage

Magic damage. A mage's battle resource is **Mana**.

| Subclass | Role in battle | Signature skills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Archmage** | Pure magic destruction and protective barriers. | Destructive Charge, Magic Barrier, Spell Storm, Arc of Destruction |
| **Sorcerer** | Curses, mirages and draining — weakens the enemy team. | Mirage, Mark of Vulnerability, Curse of Weakness, Wave of Curses |
| **Healer** | Keeps the team alive: heals, cleanses and punishes with light. | Healing, Renewal, Group Restoration, Cleansing Wave, Great Healing |
| **Enchanter** | Seals and domes: silences, shackles and shields. | Seal of Silence, Quick Barrier, Seal of Shackles, Group Dome |

## Choosing a subclass

When your hero reaches level 5, the game reminds you, and the choice opens on the hero page. Each card shows the role and the skills. Click a card to look at it; the decision is confirmed by holding the button.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**A subclass is for ever.** It decides your skills, your gear and your role in the team.
{% endhint %}

Every skill of every subclass is in [Skills](../skills/README.md), every set in the [Gear Catalog](../gear/README.md). Suggested builds: [Hero Builds](../guides/hero-builds.md).
