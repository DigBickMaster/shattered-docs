# Characteristics

A hero has **six characteristics**. Every new level gives **free points** to put into them.

| Characteristic | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Vitality** | More health. Also helps your defence and helps a block to hold. |
| **Knowledge** | A bigger pool of the battle resource — Mana for a mage, Rage for a warrior. A mage also restores mana faster. |
| **Strength** | Physical damage and the power to break through a block. A warrior builds Rage faster. Your own Strength also raises how much weight you can carry. |
| **Wisdom** | Magic damage and the power to break through a block with magic. |
| **Agility** | Dodge, and accuracy against an enemy's dodge. You also act earlier in a round. |
| **Luck** | Critical strikes and defence against criticals. Helps to break through a block. |

{% hint style="info" %}
Warriors usually lean on **Strength**, mages on **Wisdom** — but every build needs **Vitality** to survive and some **Knowledge** to pay for skills. The hero page shows exactly what changes before you save.
{% endhint %}

## Combat skill points

Apart from characteristics, each level also gives **combat skill points**. They go into **combat lines** — fine-tuning of how the hero fights:

* **Critical chance** and **anti-critical**
* **Dodge** and **anti-dodge**
* **Physical** and **magic damage power**, **physical** and **magic defence**
* **Elemental power** and **elemental resistance** — Flame, Frost, Stone, Storm

These are **ratings**: what they do in a battle depends on the rating of your opponent.

## Spending points

1. Open the hero page (**C**).
2. Press **+** next to a characteristic or a combat line. The page shows what changes.
3. Press **Save**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The Circle of War lets you in only after saving: no points may hang unspent.
{% endhint %}

## Weight

Everything the hero wears and carries in the bag has **weight**. The weight limit grows with the hero's **own Strength** — the Strength from items does not count. When the bag is full, take something off or sell it in the shop.
