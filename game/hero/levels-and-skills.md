# Levels and Skills

A hero goes from **level 1** to **level 25**.

## Gaining experience

* **The Dummy** in the Circle of War — the way for a young hero. Beating the Dummy of your level when you are ready gives a new level. Training with the Dummy is open **below level 10**.
* **Duels and group battles** in the Circle of War. Bigger battles give more experience per win, and the losers get a share too. See [Battle Rewards](../circle-of-war/battle-rewards.md).
* **Dungeons** — experience for every monster killed, even more for the boss.
* **The first win of the day** in the Circle of War gives **double experience** from level 5.

## What opens on the way

| Level | What opens |
| --- | --- |
| **1** | Your first skill — **Magic Arrow** for a mage, **Furious Strike** for a warrior |
| **5** | Your **subclass** and its first skills · the first battle **pocket** · the double-XP first win of the day · the **Amber Grotto** |
| **10** | New subclass skills · the **Dark Mines** · from now on you fight duels and group battles instead of the Dummy |
| **15** | Your **element** and **path** · your **profession** · new skills · gear with **sockets** · three pockets · the **Forgotten Fortress** · your basic strike now also hits the target's neighbours · from Phase 2: your **wallet** and everything **Amber** |
| **20** | The last tier of skills · gear with two sockets |
| **25** | The highest level |

{% hint style="info" %}
**Coming in Phase 2:** level 15 also creates your wallet and opens everything Amber, including Amber matches in Decision Arena. See [Amber](../amber/amber.md).
{% endhint %}

## Skills

New skills open at levels **5, 10, 15 and 20**. At each of these levels you choose a skill from the options of your subclass on the hero page.

Skills come in three kinds:

* **Attack** — a strike, often with an effect: a stun, a mark, a curse, damage to several enemies.
* **Support** — a heal, a shield, a boost for yourself or your allies.
* **Passive** — always works, nothing to press.

In one turn you may use **one attack skill and one support skill**. Support skills always go first. Skills cost Mana or Rage and many have a cooldown of a few turns.

The full list for every subclass: [Skills](../skills/README.md).
