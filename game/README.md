# Welcome to Shattered Worlds

{% hint style="info" %}
_The world shattered but did not go out. Shards float inside the sphere, the Core burns at its heart, and the Veil has closed around it. You are the Keeper of one of the shards._
{% endhint %}

**Shattered Worlds** is an online game by **Electrum Decisions** that joins three games in one:

* **A hero RPG** — you lead a hero through turn-based battles, choose a subclass and an element, wear gear and learn skills.
* **A strategy** — you raise a castle on your shard, hire an army, gather resources from asteroids and fight for the map.
* **A duel game** — in the Circle of War you meet other Keepers one on one or in teams.

Every Keeper belongs to one of two factions — **Aurelion** in the north or **Umbrael** in the south — and plays for it with friends in a **legion**.

## The game in one minute

1. **Your castle** stands on your faction's half of the world. It builds one thing at a time and grows level by level.
2. **Gatherers** fly to asteroids and bring home iron, energy and crystal. Everything in the castle is built with them.
3. **Your army** — Shard Wardens, Harpooners and Spark Archers — beats monsters, holds asteroids and besieges castles.
4. **Your hero** fights in the Circle of War and in dungeons, gains levels and grows stronger.
5. **The Keeper's Path** always shows the next goal at the top of the screen. Click it and it takes you where you need to go.

{% hint style="success" %}
Shattered Worlds has no gambling. The rewards of goals and daily tasks are known in advance and are the same for everybody.
{% endhint %}
