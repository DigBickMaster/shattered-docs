# Growing Your Castle

A suggested order. The Keeper's Path and the castle quests lead the same way — and pay for it.

## Castle levels 1–3: the start
* Raise the **Castle** to level 3 — the first builds take under a minute.
* Build the three **vaults** and the **Dock** at level 2.
* At level 3: **send gatherers**, build **one barracks** (the first squad is a gift) and the **Infirmary**.

## Castle levels 4–5: the base
* Keep gatherers on asteroids all the time.
* Build the other barracks and the **Wall and Dome**.
* At level 5: the **University**, the **Legion Hall** (and join a legion), the **Scout Tower**, the **Workshop** and the **Shop**.
* Start **peaceful sciences**: Construction and Gathering pay back for the whole game.

## Castle levels 6–10: the push
* From here higher Castle levels also need the **University** and the **Wall** to keep up.
* Upgrade the **vaults** before your newbie shield ends — at Castle level 10 or after 14 days.
* Hire **tier II** troops when the University reaches level 5; research their military sciences.
* Ask the legion for **help** on every long construction.
* Castle level 10 gives a milestone reward with a **castle teleport**.

## After level 10: the long game
* The Castle grows to **level 25**; big milestones at 15, 20 and 25.
* Higher troop tiers open with the University at 10, 15 and 20.
* Higher-level asteroids need a higher University level — gather deeper as you grow.
* Your legion's territory gives a gathering bonus — gather inside it.
