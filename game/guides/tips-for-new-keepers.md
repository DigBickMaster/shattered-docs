# Tips for New Keepers

1. **Follow the Keeper's Path.** It is built to walk you through every system in the right order, and every step pays.
2. **Never leave the build queue empty.** The castle builds one thing at a time. Before you log off, start the longest construction you can afford.
3. **Keep gatherers out.** From Castle level 3, always have gatherers on an asteroid. Resources are the limit of everything else.
4. **Spend points at once.** Unspent characteristic or combat skill points keep you out of the Circle of War.
5. **Beat the Dummy while you can.** Below level 10 the Dummy is the fastest and safest way to level up.
6. **Take the first win of the day.** From level 5 it gives double experience.
7. **Build the Infirmary early.** Without it, every soldier who falls dies.
8. **Upgrade the vaults.** Protected stock cannot be stolen once your newbie shield is gone.
9. **Join a legion early.** Legion help takes time off every construction, and legion buffs are free for allies.
10. **Think before you attack.** Your first attack or scout on another player ends your newbie shield for ever and starts War Frenzy.
11. **Choose for ever, carefully.** Subclass at 5, element and profession at 15 — read the cards first.
12. **Use speed-ups on the long timers.** A 5-minute speed-up matters little on a 1-minute build and a lot on the last minutes of a long one.
