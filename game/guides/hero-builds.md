# Hero Builds

These are **suggested directions**, not rules. The hero page shows exactly what each point changes before you save — use it to test your own ideas.

{% hint style="info" %}
Every build needs **Vitality** to survive and enough **Knowledge** to pay for skills. The rest goes into the characteristic that powers your damage or your role.
{% endhint %}

## Warriors

### Guardian — the wall
* **Characteristics:** Vitality first, then Strength. Some Luck helps against critical strikes through your block.
* **Combat lines:** physical defence, anti-critical.
* **Skills:** Iron Defence or Shield Bash at 5, **Taunt** at 10, **Cover of the Line** or **Mass Taunt** at 15.
* **Set:** set B (defence) of your path.

### Berserker — the damage
* **Characteristics:** Strength first, Vitality second, Luck for critical strikes.
* **Combat lines:** physical damage power, critical chance.
* **Skills:** **Bloodlust** at 5 to heal through fights, **Frenzy** or **Armour Crush** at 10, **Bloody Onslaught** at 15.
* **Set:** set A (attack) of your path.

### Hunter — the marksman
* **Characteristics:** Strength and Agility, then Vitality.
* **Combat lines:** anti-dodge, critical chance.
* **Skills:** **Aimed Shot** at 5, **Volley** at 10 for groups or **Blinding Arrow** for duels, **Group Mark** at 15.
* **Set:** set A of your path. Remember the matching quiver.

### Standard Bearer — the leader
* **Characteristics:** Vitality and Strength evenly, Knowledge to keep Rage for banners.
* **Combat lines:** physical defence, anti-critical.
* **Skills:** **Under the Banner** at 5, **Cry of Onslaught** at 10, **Banner of Onslaught** or **Banner of Fortitude** at 15.
* **Best in:** group battles — banners boost the whole team.

## Mages

### Archmage — the destroyer
* **Characteristics:** Wisdom first, then Knowledge and Vitality.
* **Combat lines:** magic damage power, critical chance, your element's power.
* **Skills:** **Destructive Charge** at 5, **Breach** or **Spell Storm** at 10, **Arc of Destruction** at 15.
* **Set:** set A of your path.

### Sorcerer — the curse
* **Characteristics:** Wisdom and Knowledge, then Vitality.
* **Combat lines:** anti-dodge so curses land, magic damage power.
* **Skills:** **Mark of Vulnerability** at 5, **Doom Seal** at 10, **Mass Vulnerability** or **Wave of Curses** at 15.
* **Best in:** teams — every ally hits harder on a cursed target.

### Healer — the life of the team
* **Characteristics:** Wisdom and Knowledge (a big mana pool), Vitality to stay up.
* **Combat lines:** magic defence, anti-critical.
* **Skills:** **Healing** at 5, **Renewal** at 10, **Triple Healing** or **Group Restoration** at 15.
* **Set:** set B of your path keeps you alive to keep healing.

### Enchanter — the shield
* **Characteristics:** Wisdom and Knowledge, then Vitality.
* **Combat lines:** magic defence, anti-dodge.
* **Skills:** **Quick Barrier** at 5, **Dome** or **Seal of Shackles** at 10, **Group Dome** at 15.
* **Best in:** group battles — shields on the whole team.
