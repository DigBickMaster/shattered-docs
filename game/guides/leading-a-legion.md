# Leading a Legion

## Founding
* **Create** the legion, choose a clear **name** and **tag**.
* Write the **description** — what the legion is about, whom it is looking for and when it gathers. It is what players of your side see in the list.
* Decide: **open** to everybody of your side, or **by application**. Open grows fast; by application keeps the company you choose.

## Officers
* Name up to **8 officers (R4)**. They accept applications, write the notice, build beacons and spend the treasury — so you are not the bottleneck.
* Use **R1–R3** to show trust and activity inside the legion.
* If you step back, **hand the legion over** — you stay on as an officer.

## Every day
* Keep the **notice** fresh: what to build, when to gather, whom to help.
* Remind members to press **Help everyone** — it costs nothing and saves everybody time.
* Members with the **Foreman** and **Quartermaster** professions buff legion allies for free — make sure everybody knows who they are.

## Every week
* Look at the **weekly goal** and tell members how to add to it — gathering, monsters or territory battles. Only those who add get the reward.
* Ask members to give their **weekly due** to the treasury.
* Check the **contribution points** table and thank the most active.

## The Legion Castle and territory
* Save the treasury for the **Legion Castle** and found it on an asteroid of your side.
* Extend the territory with **beacons**, keep them connected to the Castle, and research the legion sciences that widen it.
* Watch for **sieges** of beacons and the Castle — the legion window shows the time left. Send armies before it falls.
* Gather inside your territory for the legion's bonus.
