# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Agility** | Characteristic: dodge, accuracy against dodge, acting earlier in a round. |
| **AI agent** | An AI that plays a Keeper's account, set up by the owner, under the same rules as everybody. Your own agent is free in Phase 1 and Phase 2; our AI connection comes at release. |
| **Alarm** | A warning that an enemy army or scout is on its way to your castle, army or gatherers. |
| **Amber** | The living energy of the shattered world. From Phase 2 also the in-game currency: a crypto token in your own wallet, opened at hero level 15. |
| **Amber fee** | The 1% of every Amber match, taken in Amber. |
| **Amber match** | A Decision Arena match with an Amber entry, from hero level 15. |
| **Amberwraith** | A map monster drawn to Amber. |
| **Asteroid** | A drifting piece of the old world holding iron, energy and crystal. Levels 1–6. |
| **Aurelion** | The northern faction. Blue. |
| **Beacon** | A legion building on an asteroid that extends the legion's territory. |
| **Block** | The zone you cover in a turn. A block in the right zone stops most of a blow. |
| **Bridgehead** | An asteroid your army holds until you call it home. |
| **Castle quest** | A milestone of your domain that pays by itself. |
| **Circle of War** | Vedar Highnoon's house over the Core, where heroes fight. |
| **Combat lines** | Ratings like critical chance, dodge, damage power and resistances, raised with combat skill points. |
| **Contribution** | Your share of your side's points in a battle: damage, healing, shields, prevented damage, boosts, control. |
| **Core** | The burning heart of the sphere. The deeper towards it, the richer and the more dangerous. |
| **Decision Arena** | The one-on-one card duel of the Electrum Decisions world; joins Shattered Worlds in Phase 2. |
| **Dock** | The castle building where all troops and gatherers stand. |
| **Dozed off** | Missed a turn by not ending it within 60 seconds. |
| **Dummy** | A training fighter of your level in the Circle of War, open below level 10. |
| **Element** | Flame, Frost, Stone or Storm — chosen at level 15. |
| **First win of the day** | Your first Circle of War win of the day gives double experience from level 5. |
| **Gatherers** | Workers that mine asteroids and bring resources home. |
| **Gnome** | An NFT of the Electrum Decisions world. Not in the game yet. |
| **Infirmary** | Where your fallen soldiers are healed while there are beds. |
| **Injury** | A penalty to random characteristics after falling in a battle with players. |
| **Keeper** | A player. Every shard needs its own Keeper. |
| **Keeper's Path** | The chain of goals that guides your first days. |
| **Legion** | A company of Keepers of one faction. |
| **Legion Castle** | A legion's own castle on an asteroid, the centre of its territory. |
| **Mana** | A mage's battle resource. |
| **March** | A squad on its way across the map. |
| **Mercenary** | A profession: joins other players' battles for a price. |
| **Newbie shield** | Protection of a new castle until Castle level 10 or 14 days. |
| **Path** | The title a hero gets from subclass and element, e.g. Frost Lord, Root Healer. |
| **Pathfinder** | The badge of a Phase 1 participant, received right away on registering for Phase 2 through the invitation link. |
| **Phase 1 / Phase 2** | The two test phases, each on a brand-new world: Shattered Worlds on its own / Shattered Worlds with Decision Arena and Amber. |
| **Pocket** | A slot for a battle item; one pocket item per turn. |
| **Profession** | A hero's trade, chosen at level 15. |
| **Rage** | A warrior's battle resource, built up in the fight. |
| **Release castle** | A unique castle, shown as a unique castle skin, for Keepers who take part in Phase 2 and continue to the release. An NFT that cannot be sold or transferred; it stays for ever on the account that received it. |
| **Seals** | The hero's money. |
| **Shard** | A piece of the shattered world. Your castle stands on yours. |
| **Socket** | A place in level 15+ gear for a socket stone. |
| **Speed-up** | Shortens a castle timer: 5 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours. |
| **Subclass** | A hero's role chosen at level 5. |
| **Syra's Treasury** | Amber staking, coming in Phase 2. Not the legion treasury. |
| **Tala the Incorruptible** | The keeper of the shop. |
| **Umbrael** | The southern faction. Red. |
| **Vedar Highnoon** | Mentor of the Circle of War. |
| **Veil** | The wall of energy that splits the sphere into north and south. No army marches through it. |
| **Voidborn Guardian** | A monster of the deep that falls only to a group of players. |
| **Wallet** | Your own non-custodial wallet, created and bound to your account at hero level 15. |
| **War Frenzy** | 15 minutes after any aggression: no teleport and no shield. |
| **Weekly goal** | The goal a legion reaches together each week. |
