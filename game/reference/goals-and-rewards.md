# Goals and Rewards

The game always has a next step for you. Rewards are **known in advance** and the same for everybody.

## The Keeper's Path

A chain of goals for your first days. The current one is always at the top of the screen — **click it** and the game takes you there. Every step pays **Seals** and **speed-ups**.

1. Upgrade the Castle to level 2, then to level 3.
2. Spend your attribute points, then your combat skill points.
3. Beat the Dummy at the Circle of War and reach level 2 — then level 5.
4. Choose your subclass.
5. Clear the **Amber Grotto**, alone or with a party.
6. Fight a **5 × 5** battle at the Circle of War.
7. Build any barracks — the first squad is a gift.
8. Send gatherers to an asteroid and wait for the cargo.
9. Defeat a monster on the map.
10. Buy an item in Tala's shop.
11. Join a legion or found your own.
12. Build the Castle to level 5, reach level 6.

When the Path is done, daily tasks and achievements lead on.

## Daily tasks

A new set every day — for example:

* fight 3 battles in the Circle of War (a loss counts too);
* win 3 battles of any kind;
* fight a group battle;
* defeat 3 monsters in a dungeon;
* defeat a monster on the map;
* bring home a gathering cargo.

Each button leads where the task is done. Taking the daily rewards **day after day** builds a **streak**; miss a day and the streak starts again.

## Achievements

Taken in any order, each once. A medallion shows the rank. Examples:

* **Hero of level 10, 15, 20** · **Castle of level 10**
* **First win over a player** · **25 wins over players**
* **10, 100, 500 battles won** · **10 group battles**
* **10 and 50 dungeons cleared**
* **25 gathering trips** · **25 map monsters defeated**

## Castle quests
