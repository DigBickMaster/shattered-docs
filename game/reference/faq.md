# FAQ

<details>

<summary>I don't know what to do next.</summary>

Look at the top of the screen: the **Keeper's Path** always shows the next goal. Click it and the game takes you there.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why can't I enter the Circle of War?</summary>

You have unspent points. Open the hero page (**C**), spend your characteristic and combat skill points and press **Save**. A **heavy injury** also keeps you out of battles — see [Injuries and Healing](../hero/injuries-and-healing.md).

</details>

<details>

<summary>The Dummy is gone. Where do I train now?</summary>

Training with the Dummy is open below level 10. From level 10 your fights are **duels** and **group battles**.

</details>

<details>

<summary>My group battle request doesn't start.</summary>

A group battle starts only when **both sides are full** and everybody has confirmed the ready check. Wait for more heroes of your level, or join a request that is already filling up.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why can't I send gatherers?</summary>

Gathering opens at **Castle level 3**. Higher-level asteroids also need the University science **Deep Gathering**, and an asteroid held by another player's army must be taken first.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can someone attack me right away?</summary>

No. A new castle has a **newbie shield** until Castle level 10 or for 14 days — but it falls at once if **you** attack or scout another player. Your armies and gatherers out on the map are not shielded.

</details>

<details>

<summary>My soldiers died after a battle.</summary>

The fallen go to the **Infirmary** only while it has free beds; beyond the beds they die. Build the Infirmary higher and heal the wounded quickly.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I change my subclass, element or profession?</summary>

No. They are chosen once and for ever — so are the faction and the class.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Can I give an item to a friend?</summary>

No. Items cannot be passed between players. Players trade profession goods on the **market**.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Why can't I teleport?</summary>

You are in **War Frenzy** — for 15 minutes after any attack or scout. The time left is shown by the teleport button.

</details>
