# Amber

The in-game currency — coming into Shattered Worlds in Phase 2.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Coming in Phase 2.** Amber opens at hero level 15. See [Phases](../getting-started/phases.md).
{% endhint %}

**Amber** is the in-game currency of the Electrum Decisions world. It is a **crypto token**: it is kept in your own wallet, not by the studio, and it is traded only on decentralised exchanges (DEXes) — not on custodial exchanges.

## Amber in short

* **The game does not sell Amber.** You cannot buy it in the app or in the game shop.
* **Buying, selling and sending Amber happen only outside the game** — through your personal cabinet on the website, at your own responsibility. See [Your Wallet](your-wallet.md).
* There are **no Amber transfers inside the game.**

## Everything Amber opens at level 15

At **hero level 15** — the level of your element and profession — the game creates your **wallet** and opens everything Amber: Amber matches in Decision Arena and spending Amber in the game.

## Where a Keeper gets Amber

| Way                 | How                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Amber matches**   | Win Amber matches in Decision Arena. See [Amber Matches and Tournaments](../decision-arena/amber-matches-and-tournaments.md). |
| **Your own wallet** | Send Amber you already have to your wallet from the website.                                                             |

## What Amber is for

* **Upgrades and speed-ups** of your own account.
* **Amber matches** in Decision Arena.
* Not everything in the game can be bought with Amber.

{% hint style="info" %}
**The game sells time, never power.** Amber can make you faster, not stronger than the rules allow.
{% endhint %}

## Syra's Treasury

**Syra's Treasury** is Amber **staking**. It receives 3% of every Amber match. How it works will be described here when it opens in Phase 2.

Syra's Treasury has nothing to do with your legion's [treasury](../legions/the-treasury.md), which holds iron, energy and crystal.

## What the game never does

* It does not promise what Amber will be worth.
* It does not show prices of Amber or links to buy it.
* It does not hold your keys.
