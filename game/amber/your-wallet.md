# Your Wallet

A wallet of your own, bound to your account at hero level 15.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Coming in Phase 2.** See [Phases](../getting-started/phases.md).
{% endhint %}

## Your wallet

* When your hero reaches **level 15**, the game creates a **wallet** with **thirdweb** and binds it to your account and your **e-mail**.
* It is **non-custodial**: the keys belong **only to you**. Neither the studio nor thirdweb keeps them.

{% hint style="info" %}
Add your e-mail in [Settings](../community/settings-and-account.md) before level 15 — the wallet is bound to it.
{% endhint %}

## Transfers happen on the website, not in the game

The game apps cannot use crypto, so the game itself has **no transfers** and no Amber purchases. Everything Amber is done in your **personal cabinet on the website**. There you find how to:

1. connect an outside wallet such as MetaMask or Trust Wallet;
2. buy POL with a card;
3. swap it for Amber on a decentralised exchange;
4. send Amber to your game wallet — and back.

{% hint style="danger" %}
**You carry the responsibility** for what you do with your wallet and your Amber outside the game. Never share your keys or your password. The game staff will never ask for them.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
Items, Seals and castle resources still **cannot be passed between players** — see [Seals and Resources](../economy/seals-and-resources.md). Wallets are only for Amber.
{% endhint %}
