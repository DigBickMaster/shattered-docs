# Standard Bearer Gear

The first set of the Standard Bearer. It opens after you choose the subclass, piece by piece from level 5 to level 12.

## Level 15–17 · element sets

From level 15 every path has **two sets**: **A** leans to **attack**, **B** leans to **defence**. A complete set gives a **set bonus** from level 17.

| Element | Path | Set A — attack | Set B — defence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flame | Banner of Onslaught | Banner of Onslaught: Spark | Banner of Onslaught: Decay |
| Frost | Banner of Fate | Banner of Fate: Hoarfrost | Banner of Fate: Rime |
| Stone | Banner of the Wall | Banner of the Wall: Rubble | Banner of the Wall: Rock |
| Storm | Banner of the Wind | Banner of the Wind: Gust | Banner of the Wind: Peal |

## Level 20–23 · element sets

From level 20 every path has **two sets**: **A** leans to **attack**, **B** leans to **defence**. A complete set gives a **set bonus** from level 22.

| Element | Path | Set A — attack | Set B — defence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flame | Banner of Onslaught | Banner of Onslaught: Ash | Banner of Onslaught: Heat |
| Frost | Banner of Fate | Banner of Fate: Chill | Banner of Fate: Glaze |
| Stone | Banner of the Wall | Banner of the Wall: Granite | Banner of the Wall: Obsidian |
| Storm | Banner of the Wind | Banner of the Wind: Thunder | Banner of the Wind: Squall |
