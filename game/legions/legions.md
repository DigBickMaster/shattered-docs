# Legions

A **legion** is a company of Keepers of one faction. Legions help each other build, fill a shared treasury, raise a **Legion Castle**, hold **territory** and reach a **weekly goal** together.

## Finding a legion

Open the legion window and go to the **Legions** tab: the list of all legions of your side, with search by name or tag. Click a legion to open its **card** — its head, members, level and what it says about itself.

* **Open legions** — anybody of your side can join at once.
* **By application** — you send an application, and the head or an officer decides. You can have several applications at a time and withdraw them.

## Founding a legion

Press **Create legion**, give it a **name** (3–20 characters) and a short **tag**. You become its head.

## Ranks

| Rank | Who | What they can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **R5 · head** | The founder or whoever the legion was handed over to | Everything: gives ranks, hands the legion over, and everything officers do |
| **R4 · officer** | Up to 8 per legion | Accept and decline applications, open or close joining, remove or promote members below them, write the notice and the description, found and upgrade the Legion Castle, start legion sciences, build beacons |
| **R3, R2, R1** | Members | Take part in everything else. A new member starts at R1 |

The head can **hand the legion over** to another member — the old head becomes an officer.

## The notice and the description

* **The notice** is for members only: gatherings, plans, requests — what to build, whom to help.
* **The description** is public for your side: what the legion is about and whom it is looking for. It shows in the list of legions and on the card.

## Members

The **Members** tab shows everybody's rank and who is **in the game** right now. Officers and the head can raise or lower ranks and remove members.

## Leaving

Press **Leave the legion** and confirm.

## What a legion gives

* **Help** with constructions and research — see [Legion Help and the Weekly Goal](legion-help-and-weekly-goal.md).
* **Free buffs** from legion allies with the **Foreman** and **Quartermaster** professions (others pay for them). A **Shaman** buffs any player of your faction.
* **The legion chat** — only members read it.
* **The Legion Castle, its sciences and territory** — see [The Legion Castle and Territory](legion-castle-and-territory.md).
