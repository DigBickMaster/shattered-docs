# The Legion Castle and Territory

## The Legion Castle

A legion can raise its own **Legion Castle** on an asteroid of its side of the world.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Found it

The head or an officer picks an asteroid on the map → **Who is here** → **Found the Legion Castle**. The cost is paid from the treasury. The asteroid becomes the Legion Castle and leaves the map as an asteroid.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Upgrade it

Each level is paid from the treasury and takes time to build. Only the head and the officers upgrade it.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

The Legion Castle is the centre of the legion's **territory**.

## Territory and beacons

* The territory is a **sphere** around the Legion Castle.
* The head or an officer extends it with **beacons**: an asteroid inside the territory → **Who is here** → **Build a beacon**.
* A beacon must stay connected to the Castle. A beacon **cut off** from the Castle holds nothing.
* Asteroids inside the territory form **clusters**, and gathering there gives the legion's members a **bonus**.
* The legion window shows the radius, the beacons, the asteroids, the clusters and **foreign gatherers** on your territory.

## Sieges of beacons and Legion Castles

* A foreign army that **holds a beacon's asteroid** long enough **brings the beacon down**.
* A Legion Castle can be **besieged and destroyed** the same way.
* The legion window shows a running siege and how long is left — time to send armies.
* Battles on these asteroids, attacking or defending, count for the weekly goal **Battles for the territory**.

## Legion sciences

The Legion Castle opens **legion sciences**. They are paid from the treasury, researched one at a time and started by the head or an officer. They widen the spheres of the Legion Castle and the beacons, and **every member gets the bonus**.
