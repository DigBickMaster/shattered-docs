# Legion Help and the Weekly Goal

## Legion help

When you build or research, you can **ask your legion for help**.

* You need the **Legion Hall** in your castle.
* Every member can help once — each help takes **a minute off** the timer.
* The Legion Hall level sets how many helps one construction or research can receive.
* Hiring troops takes no help.
* **Help everyone** sends your help to every request at once.

The **Help** tab shows your request — how many have helped — and the requests of others.

## The weekly goal

Every week the legion gets **one goal** to reach together. It can be:

| Goal | What counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Gather resources** | Resources the members' gatherers bring home (crystal counts more) |
| **Defeat monsters** | Victories of the members' armies over monsters on the map |
| **Battles for the territory** | Battles on the asteroids of legion beacons and Legion Castles — attacking or defending |

* The **Week** tab shows the goal, the progress bar, the reward and how long is left in the week.
* It also shows **your part of the goal** — in the goal's own units.
* When the goal is reached, **everybody who added to it** gets the reward — speed-ups. If you did not add, there is no reward for you.

## Contribution points

Below the goal, the **Week** tab shows the members' **contribution points** — this week and all time. Points are the legion's own count, separate from the goal: they come from giving to the treasury, helping members and fighting for beacons and the Legion Castle.
