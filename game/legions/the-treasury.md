# The Treasury

The **legion treasury** holds resources given by the members. It pays for the **Legion Castle** and for **legion sciences**.

## Giving to the treasury

Open the **Treasury** tab and give iron, energy or crystal.

* Each week there is a **weekly due** — the tab shows whether you have given yours.
* You can always give more than the due.
* What you give earns you **contribution points** in the legion's weekly table.

## Who spends it

The **head** and the **officers (R4)** spend the treasury: they found and upgrade the Legion Castle and start legion sciences. If the treasury is short, the window says so — that is the moment for members to give.
