# How a Battle Works

Battles in Shattered Worlds are **turn-based**. The same rules apply in the Circle of War and in dungeons.

## A turn

Every turn each fighter chooses at the same time:

1. **A strike zone** — where to hit: **Head**, **Torso**, **Waist** or **Legs**.
2. **A block zone** — what to cover.
3. Optionally **one attack skill** and **one support skill**.
4. Optionally **one item from a pocket**.

Then press **End the turn**. When everybody has chosen, the round is played out and the battle log tells what happened.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Guess where the enemy strikes** — your block takes the blow. **Hit where the enemy is not covered** — your strike lands.
{% endhint %}

## The order of a round

* **Support comes first** — heals, shields and boosts go before the strikes.
* **Agility** decides who acts earlier among the strikes.
* Pocket grenades fly as an extra strike before your own.

## The turn timer

A turn has **60 seconds**. If you do not press **End the turn** in time, you **doze off** and miss the turn — and the enemy strikes unanswered. Watch the timer.

## What decides a strike

* **Damage** — from your weapon, Strength or Wisdom, and your skills.
* **Defence** — physical and magic defence reduce what comes through.
* **Dodge and accuracy** — an agile fighter may avoid a strike; anti-dodge works against that.
* **Critical strikes** — a lucky strike hits much harder and can break through a block; anti-critical works against that.
* **Blocks** — a block in the right zone stops most of a blow, but strong Strength, Wisdom or a critical strike can break through.
* **Elements** — from level 15 elemental power and resistance come into play.

## Rage and Mana

* A **warrior** builds **Rage** during the fight and spends it on skills.
* A **mage** spends **Mana** on skills and restores some of it every round.

## Group battles

In a team every fighter has their own turn and their own target. Choose your target on the enemy side. Healers and Standard Bearers aim their support at allies.

From level 15 a hero's basic strike also hits the main target's neighbours.

## Pockets in battle

One pocket item per turn: an **HP Stone**, a **Mana Stone**, a **Shield Stone**, a **grenade** or a **scroll**. Each item has its own uses and cooldown. See [Gear and Inventory](../hero/gear-and-inventory.md).

## Mercenaries

During a duel or a group battle a player can call a **Mercenary** — another player with that profession — to join on their side for an agreed price. See [Professions](../economy/professions.md).

## The end of the battle

When one side has nobody left standing, the battle ends. The end screen shows the result, the experience and the Seals, and each fighter's share of the side's contribution.
