# The Circle of War

The **Circle of War** is held in the house of **Vedar Highnoon** over the Core. Here heroes of both factions fight one on one and in teams. Open it with **To battle** on the map.

## What you can do here

* **Train with the Dummy** — a fighter of your level that strikes fair and takes no offence. Open **below level 10**. Vedar's advice: the best way to start.
* **Duel** — challenge a hero of your level who is in the game, or accept a challenge.
* **Group battles** — create a request or join one, and fight in a team.
* **Watch** a battle that is going on.
* See **who is in the Circle** right now — click a portrait to open the hero's page.
* Look at your **past battles**, your **wins and losses** and your **win streak**.

## Battle formats

| Format | Size | Opens at | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Duel** | 1 × 1 | level 1 | Pure skill: one on one. |
| **Squad** | 5 × 5 | level 5 | Roles and a common goal. |
| **Wall** | 10 × 10 | level 10 | A battle of legions. |
| **Battle** | 20 × 20 | level 15 | A great battle. |

The bigger the format, the more experience a win gives.

## Requests

1. Choose a format. The list shows requests of **your level** — or all of them, with the level filter.
2. **Join** a request on the side with a free place, or **create** your own with a level range.
3. When the request is full, everybody gets a **ready check**. When all confirm, the battle starts.

{% hint style="info" %}
A group battle starts only when **both sides are full**. If somebody does not confirm in time, the request opens again for others.
{% endhint %}

## Duels and challenges

Duels are fought between heroes of **the same level**. Send a challenge to a hero who is in the game; when they accept, the duel starts at once. Challenges sent to you arrive in **Incoming**.

## Rematch series

After a duel or a group battle the sides can agree to a **rematch**. A series of rematches is counted together, and the winner of the series is shown on the end screen.

## The first win of the day

From level 5, your **first win of the day** in the Circle of War gives **double experience**. The Circle shows it as "waiting for you" until you take it.
