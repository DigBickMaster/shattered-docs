# Battle Rewards

## Experience

* **Every side gets experience.** The winners share most of the battle's experience; the losers share the rest. Nobody leaves a real battle with nothing.
* **Your share depends on your contribution** — damage, healing, shields, prevented damage, boosts, control, and what your armour and blocks kept off. The end screen shows your contribution and your share of your side.
* **Bigger formats give more experience** per win.
* **The first win of the day** gives double experience from level 5.
* With the Dummy, beating a Dummy of your level when you are ready gives a new level.

{% hint style="info" %}
Training fighters that fill empty places in a group battle do not take experience, and battles filled with them do not count in your win record.
{% endhint %}

## Seals

Won battles in the Circle of War — with the Dummy, duels and group battles — pay **Seals**, up to a daily number of paid wins.

## Records

Your **wins and losses**, your **win streak** and your **best streak** are shown in the Circle and on your public hero page.

## Achievements

Battles move achievements such as **First win over a player**, **10 group battles**, **100 battles won** and more. See [Goals and Rewards](../reference/goals-and-rewards.md).
