# Sorcerer

**Mage subclass.** Curses, mirages and draining. The Sorcerer weakens the enemy team so that allies win the fight.

At levels **5, 10 and 15** you choose **one skill of three**. At level **20** you choose one more active skill and one **passive**. Choose on the hero page; the cards in the game show the exact numbers.

## Level 5

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mark of Vulnerability** | Attack | The target takes more damage from everyone for a couple of rounds. |
| **Mirage** | Attack | The target misses more for a couple of rounds. |
| **Blight** | Attack | The target deals less damage for a couple of rounds. |

## Level 10

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Doom Seal** | Attack | A hit that also makes the target more vulnerable. |
| **Withering** | Attack | A hit that burns part of the target's Mana or Rage. |
| **Curse of Weakness** | Passive | After the Sorcerer's magic hit, the target deals less damage for a round. |

## Level 15

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Wave of Curses** | Attack | Up to three enemies deal less damage for a couple of rounds. |
| **Mass Vulnerability** | Attack | Up to three enemies take more damage for a couple of rounds. |
| **Draining Circle** | Attack | Strikes up to three enemies and burns their Mana or Rage. |

## Level 20

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Star Rift** | Attack | A strong hit that lowers the target's elemental resistance for a round. |
| **Wave of Distortion** | Attack | Hits the main target and its neighbours and weakens the target's next skill. |
| **Draining Pattern** | Attack | A hit that keeps draining the target's resource for two more rounds. |

## Level 20 · passive

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Dominion of Curses** | Makes all of the Sorcerer's curses stronger. |
| **Arcane Drain** | More magic power and armour penetration. |
| **Spell Mirror** | More resistance of your element and protection against critical strikes. |
