# Hunter

**Warrior subclass.** A marksman with a bow or a crossbow. The Hunter marks targets, pins them down and strikes from afar.

At levels **5, 10 and 15** you choose **one skill of three**. At level **20** you choose one more active skill and one **passive**. Choose on the hero page; the cards in the game show the exact numbers.

## Level 5

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Aimed Shot** | Attack | A precise shot that is hard to dodge and partly gets through a block. |
| **Snare** | Attack | A shot that may stun the target for a round. |
| **Leap Back** | Support | The Hunter dodges much better for a couple of rounds. |

## Level 10

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Volley** | Attack | Arrows rain on the main target and two more enemies. |
| **Blinding Arrow** | Attack | A shot that makes the target miss more for a couple of rounds. |
| **Hunter's Mark** | Passive | After the Hunter's hit, the target dodges worse for a round. |

## Level 15

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hunter's Volley** | Attack | Hits the main target and grazes its neighbours. |
| **Binding Shot** | Attack | Shoots up to three enemies and makes them miss more for a round. |
| **Group Mark** | Attack | Marks up to three enemies: they dodge worse for a couple of rounds. |

## Level 20

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Piercing Shot** | Attack | A powerful shot that ignores part of the target's physical defence. |
| **Squall Volley** | Attack | A strong shot on the main target that also hits its neighbours. |
| **Hunting Mark** | Attack | Marks the target: it takes more damage from this Hunter for a couple of rounds. |

## Level 20 · passive

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Hunter's Eye** | More critical strikes and accuracy against dodge. |
| **Piercing Aim** | Strikes ignore more of the armour; better accuracy against dodge. |
| **Tracker's Instinct** | More attack of your element and better dodge. |
