# Standard Bearer

**Warrior subclass.** Leads the team under a banner: boosts allies, shields them and frightens enemies.

At levels **5, 10 and 15** you choose **one skill of three**. At level **20** you choose one more active skill and one **passive**. Choose on the hero page; the cards in the game show the exact numbers.

## Level 5

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Plant on the Move** | Attack | A strike after which allies deal more damage for a round. |
| **Under the Banner** | Support | Allies take less damage for a couple of rounds. |
| **Confuse** | Attack | A strike that makes the target miss more for a round. |

## Level 10

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cry of Onslaught** | Support | Allies deal more damage for a couple of rounds. |
| **Cry of Fortitude** | Passive | Using a class skill gives the chosen allies a shared barrier. |
| **Cry of Dread** | Attack | The target deals less damage for a couple of rounds. |

## Level 15

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Banner of Onslaught** | Support | The whole team deals more damage for a couple of rounds. |
| **Banner of Fortitude** | Support | The whole team takes less damage for a couple of rounds. |
| **Banner of Dread** | Attack | Up to three enemies deal less damage for a couple of rounds. |

## Level 20

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Banner of Onslaught** | Support | All living allies attack harder for a couple of rounds. |
| **Banner of Fortitude** | Support | All living allies take less damage for a couple of rounds. |
| **Banner of Dread** | Attack | A strike; the target deals less damage for a couple of rounds. |

## Level 20 · passive

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Banner of Unity** | Makes all command boosts stronger. |
| **Commander's Voice** | More health and physical defence. |
| **Unbreakable Spirit** | More resistance of your element and protection against critical strikes. |
