# Enchanter

**Mage subclass.** Seals and domes. The Enchanter shields allies, shackles enemies and breaks their barriers.

At levels **5, 10 and 15** you choose **one skill of three**. At level **20** you choose one more active skill and one **passive**. Choose on the hero page; the cards in the game show the exact numbers.

## Level 5

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Seal Strike** | Attack | Hits an enemy and gives an ally a barrier. |
| **Seal of Silence** | Attack | Gives an ally a barrier and burns part of the enemy's Mana or Rage. |
| **Quick Barrier** | Support | A barrier on an ally for a couple of rounds. |

## Level 10

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Barrier Break** | Attack | A magic hit that can turn part of your own barrier into extra damage. |
| **Seal of Shackles** | Attack | The target misses more for a round. |
| **Dome** | Passive | When an enemy breaks your barrier, a small new one appears. |

## Level 15

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Group Dome** | Support | Shields up to three allies for a couple of rounds. |
| **Circle of Shackles** | Attack | Up to three enemies miss more for a round. |
| **Barriers Break** | Attack | Strips the shields of up to three enemies and hits them. |

## Level 20

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Group Dome** | Support | Strong shields on up to three allies for a couple of rounds. |
| **Barriers Break** | Attack | Removes the target's shield, then hits hard. |
| **Circle of Vulnerability** | Attack | A hit after which the target dodges much worse for a couple of rounds. |

## Level 20 · passive

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Seals of Destruction** | Removing shields and barriers becomes stronger. |
| **Circuit of Shackles** | Stronger control effects and better accuracy against dodge. |
| **Dome of the Veil** | Stronger shields from the Enchanter and more resistance of your element. |
