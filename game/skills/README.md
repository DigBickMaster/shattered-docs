# Skills

## How skills work

* **Attack** skills strike, often with an effect — a stun, a mark, a curse, damage to several enemies.
* **Support** skills heal, shield or boost you and your allies. They always act **before** the strikes.
* **Passive** skills always work — there is nothing to press.
* In one turn you may use **one attack skill and one support skill**, plus your strike and one pocket item.
* Skills cost **Mana** (mage) or **Rage** (warrior). Most have a **cooldown** of a few turns.
* The skill card in the game shows the exact cost, cooldown and numbers.

## The first skill

| Class | Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mage** | **Magic Arrow** | A separate magic strike after the weapon strike. |
| **Warrior** | **Furious Strike** | Strengthens the weapon strike; it partly gets through a block. |

From **level 15** every hero's basic strike also grazes the main target's two neighbours.
