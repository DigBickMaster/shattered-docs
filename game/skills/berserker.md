# Berserker

**Warrior subclass.** Raw damage. The Berserker trades defence for fury and heals himself with the blood he spills.

At levels **5, 10 and 15** you choose **one skill of three**. At level **20** you choose one more active skill and one **passive**. Choose on the hero page; the cards in the game show the exact numbers.

## Level 5

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sweep** | Attack | A wide swing that hits the main target and one more enemy. |
| **Dreadful Roar** | Attack | Intimidates the target: it deals less damage for a couple of rounds. |
| **Bloodlust** | Support | For a few rounds the Berserker heals from the damage his strikes deal. |

## Level 10

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Frenzy** | Support | More physical damage for a few rounds. |
| **Armour Crush** | Attack | A strike that breaks the target's physical defence for a couple of rounds. |
| **Blood for Blood** | Passive | The Berserker's physical strikes heal him a little. |

## Level 15

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Whirlwind Strike** | Attack | Hits the main target and grazes its neighbours. |
| **Bloody Onslaught** | Attack | Strikes three enemies and heals the Berserker from the damage dealt. |
| **Chain Fury** | Attack | Weakens the damage of up to three enemies for a couple of rounds. |

## Level 20

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Berserker's Execution** | Attack | One devastating strike on a single target. |
| **Circular Rift** | Attack | A strong strike on the main target that also hits its neighbours. |
| **Bloody Onslaught** | Attack | A heavy strike that heals the Berserker for part of the health he lost. |

## Level 20 · passive

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Blood Rage** | Makes the Berserker's physical damage stronger. |
| **Untamed Onslaught** | More critical strikes and accuracy against dodge. |
| **Berserker's Endurance** | More health and resistance of your element. |
