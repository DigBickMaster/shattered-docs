# Archmage

**Mage subclass.** Pure magic destruction and protective barriers. The Archmage hits hardest of all mages.

At levels **5, 10 and 15** you choose **one skill of three**. At level **20** you choose one more active skill and one **passive**. Choose on the hero page; the cards in the game show the exact numbers.

## Level 5

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Destructive Charge** | Attack | A powerful magic charge at one enemy. |
| **Stunning Discharge** | Attack | A magic hit that may stun the target for a round. |
| **Magic Barrier** | Support | A barrier that absorbs damage for a couple of rounds. |

## Level 10

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Spell Storm** | Attack | A storm of magic spread over up to three enemies. |
| **Breach** | Attack | A hit that first breaks the enemy's barrier, then deals damage. |
| **Reflecting Sphere** | Passive | Before the first magic hit of a round, a sphere absorbs part of it and reflects some back. |

## Level 15

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Arc of Destruction** | Attack | Hits the main target and grazes its neighbours. |
| **Chain Seal** | Attack | Strikes up to three enemies, with a chance to stun. |
| **Barrier Prism** | Support | Shields up to three allies for a couple of rounds. |

## Level 20

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Elemental Rift** | Attack | A devastating hit empowered by your element. |
| **Cascade of Elements** | Attack | A strong hit on the main target that also strikes its neighbours. |
| **Seal of Destruction** | Attack | Removes the target's shield, then hits hard. |

## Level 20 · passive

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Heart of the Element** | More attack of your element. |
| **Deep Reserve** | More magic power and a bigger mana pool. |
| **Arcane Ward** | More defence and resistance of your element. |
