# Guardian

**Warrior subclass.** The wall of the team. The Guardian protects allies, pulls enemies onto himself and holds the line.

At levels **5, 10 and 15** you choose **one skill of three**. At level **20** you choose one more active skill and one **passive**. Choose on the hero page; the cards in the game show the exact numbers.

## Level 5

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Piercing Strike** | Attack | A heavy strike that partly gets through a block in the matching zone. |
| **Shield Bash** | Attack | A blow with the shield that may stun the target for a round. |
| **Iron Defence** | Support | The Guardian takes less damage for a few rounds. |

## Level 10

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ram** | Attack | A powerful strike that weakens the target's physical defence for a couple of rounds. |
| **Taunt** | Attack | For a couple of rounds the target deals less damage to everyone except the Guardian — attack him or lose power. |
| **Shield Wall** | Passive | When the Guardian holds a block, he covers an ally with a barrier. |

## Level 15

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cover of the Line** | Support | The whole team takes less damage for a couple of rounds. |
| **Mass Taunt** | Attack | Taunts up to three enemies at once: they deal less damage to anyone but the Guardian. |
| **Strike Line** | Attack | Hits the main target and grazes its neighbours. |

## Level 20

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Line Strike** | Attack | A strong strike on the main target that also hits its neighbours. |
| **Bastion's Challenge** | Attack | Forces the target to attack the Guardian next round, and gives the Guardian a shield. |
| **Living Wall** | Support | The Guardian and one ally take less damage for a couple of rounds. |

## Level 20 · passive

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Unshakable Line** | Makes all of the Guardian's protective effects stronger. |
| **Heavy Armour** | More physical defence and health. |
| **Stone Stance** | More defence of your element and protection against critical strikes. |
