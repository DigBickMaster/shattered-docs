# Healer

**Mage subclass.** Keeps the team alive. The Healer heals, cleanses negative effects and punishes enemies with light.

At levels **5, 10 and 15** you choose **one skill of three**. At level **20** you choose one more active skill and one **passive**. Choose on the hero page; the cards in the game show the exact numbers.

## Level 5

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Punishing Light** | Attack | Strikes an enemy, heals an ally and weakens the enemy's healing for a round. |
| **Blinding Flash** | Support | Heals an ally and removes one negative effect (not a stun). |
| **Healing** | Support | A strong heal for one ally. |

## Level 10

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Judgement** | Attack | A hit that weakens the target's healing for a couple of rounds. |
| **Renewal** | Support | Heals at once, then keeps healing for a few more rounds. |
| **Blessing** | Passive | The Healer's heals also leave a small barrier. |

## Level 15

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Triple Healing** | Support | Heals three allies at once. |
| **Cleansing Wave** | Support | Heals up to three allies and removes a negative effect from each. |
| **Group Restoration** | Support | Heals up to three allies at once and again next round. |

## Level 20

| Skill | Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Great Healing** | Support | A very strong heal for one living ally. |
| **Circle of Renewal** | Support | Heals up to three living allies. |
| **Cleansing Tide** | Support | Heals an ally and removes their oldest negative effect (not a stun). |

## Level 20 · passive

| Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Healer's Heart** | Makes the Healer's healing stronger. |
| **Vital Reserve** | More magic power and a bigger mana pool. |
| **Life Charm** | Stronger shields from the Healer and more defence of your element. |
