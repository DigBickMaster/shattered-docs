# The Shattered World

The world shattered — but it did not go out.

What was once whole is now a **sphere of shards** hanging in the dark. At its heart burns the **Core**, the source of the light that still holds everything together. Around it the **Veil** has closed: a wall of energy that splits the sphere into two halves and that no army can march through.

Between the shards drift **asteroids** — pieces of the old world, rich in iron, energy and crystal. The deeper you go towards the Core, the richer the asteroids are — and the more dangerous the creatures that guard them.

## The Keepers

When the world shattered, **four Keepers** held the shards by the Core and kept the sphere from falling apart. Their houses still stand over the Core.

Since then every shard needs a Keeper of its own. **You are one of them.** Your shard carries your castle, your hero is your hand in battle, and your legion is the company you keep.

Two of the old Keepers you will meet early:

* **Vedar Highnoon** — mentor of the Circle of War. His house over the Core holds the Circle, where heroes fight one on one and in squads. He is the first voice you hear in the game.
* **Tala the Incorruptible** — keeper of the shop. _"One price for all. No haggling — and no cheating. What you buy is what you get."_

## Amber

**Amber** is the living energy of the shattered world. It glows in the arrows of the Spark Archers, in the hollows of the Amber Grotto and in the dome that rises over a well-defended castle. Where Amber gathers, creatures gather too — the **Amberwraiths** are drawn to it.
