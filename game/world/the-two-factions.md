# The Two Factions

Every Keeper chooses a faction when creating a hero. **The choice is for ever.**

{% columns %}
{% column %}
### Aurelion

The **northern** half of the world. Its colour is **blue**.

Aurelion castles stand in the north, and its Keepers gather on the northern asteroids.
{% endcolumn %}

{% column %}
### Umbrael

The **southern** half of the world. Its colour is **red**.

Umbrael castles stand in the south, and its Keepers gather on the southern asteroids.
{% endcolumn %}
{% endcolumns %}

## What the faction decides

* **Where your castle stands** — on your faction's half of the sphere.
* **The colour of your interface.**
* **Who your allies are.** Legions are formed inside one faction. Buffs from professions such as the Foreman and the Quartermaster are given to your own side only.
* **Who your enemies are.** Sieges and battles for asteroids are fought across the faction line.

## What the faction does not decide

* **The world chat is shared by both factions.** You can talk to anyone in the world.
* **The Circle of War is open to everyone.** Heroes of both factions meet there.

{% hint style="info" %}
The game keeps the factions balanced. When one side has many more Keepers, creating new heroes on that side can be closed for a while.
{% endhint %}
