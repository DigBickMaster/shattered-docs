# Castle Quests and Speed-ups

## Castle quests

Castle quests are milestones of your domain: build the Castle to the next level, build each vault, the University, the barracks, the Infirmary, the Scout Tower, the Legion Hall, the Workshop, the Shop and the Dock, send your first gatherers and win your first fight against a monster.

* They **pay by themselves** as soon as they are done — the moment a construction finishes.
* Rewards are resources, and some quests also give gatherers or the first squad of troops.
* Big milestones come at **Castle levels 10, 15, 20 and 25**. The Castle level 10 milestone also gives a **castle teleport**.

The **Show** button on a quest opens the castle where it is done.

## Speed-ups

A **speed-up** shortens a running castle timer: construction, research, hiring or healing. Speed-ups come in three sizes — **5 minutes**, **1 hour** and **8 hours**.

Use them in the castle: **Speed up** on the queues panel.

Where speed-ups come from:

* Keeper's Path rewards and castle quests;
* dungeon chests and bosses;
* the legion's weekly goal;
* the shop.

Legion help is a free way to take time off constructions and research. See [Legion Help](../legions/legion-help-and-weekly-goal.md).
{% endhint %}
