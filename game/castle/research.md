# Research

The **University** opens at Castle level 5. It researches **sciences**:

* **Military sciences** strengthen your troops — their attack, defence and health.
* **Peaceful sciences** speed up construction, gathering and healing.
* **Troop tiers** — new, stronger tiers of every kind of troop. The higher the tier, the stronger and the dearer the troops.

## The science tree

The University has **five tiers**. Tier I is open from the start; tiers II, III, IV and V open at University levels **5, 10, 15 and 20**. Every science goes up to **level 10**.

### Military sciences

For each of the three troops — **Shard Wardens**, **Harpooners**, **Spark Archers**:

| Science | What it improves |
| --- | --- |
| **Attack** | The attack of that troop |
| **Defence** | The defence of that troop |
| **Speed** | How fast that troop marches |

A military science of tier N works on the troops of **tier N** of its kind — so as you hire higher tiers, research the matching tier.

### Peaceful sciences

| Science | What it improves |
| --- | --- |
| **Construction** | Every construction is faster |
| **Hiring** | Hiring of troops and gatherers is faster |
| **Gathering** | Gathering on asteroids is faster |
| **Healing** | Healing in the Infirmary is faster |

To research a science of a higher tier, research the same science of the tier before it first.

## How research works

* New tiers of sciences open with the levels of the **Castle** and the **University**.
* Every science has its own levels. The tree shows a line lit up when the way to a science is open.
* A science of tier N works on the troops of tier N of its kind.
* A closed tier can still be opened and looked at — you see what to build for.
* The University researches **one science at a time**.

Some sciences matter for the map: for example, gathering on higher-level asteroids needs the science **Deep Gathering**.

## Legion sciences

A legion has its own sciences, paid from the **legion treasury**. They widen the reach of the Legion Castle and the beacons, and every member gets their bonus. They are started by the head and the officers. See [The Legion Castle and Territory](../legions/legion-castle-and-territory.md).
