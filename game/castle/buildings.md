# Buildings

Buildings open with the level of the **Main Castle**.

| Building | Opens at Castle | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Main Castle** | 1 | The heart of the domain. Its level opens new buildings and sciences. |
| **Wall and Dome** | 2 | Defends the castle. The energy dome grows stronger with every level. |
| **Dock** | 2 | Where all your troops and gatherers stand. Click it to see your whole army: how many fighters and gatherers and where they are. |
| **Warehouse: Iron Vault** | 2 | Protects iron from looting. |
| **Warehouse: Energy Vault** | 2 | Protects energy from looting. |
| **Warehouse: Crystal Vault** | 2 | Protects crystal from looting. |
| **Shard Warden Barracks** | 3 | Hires Shard Wardens — the shield wall, the base of any army. |
| **Harpooner Barracks** | 3 | Hires Harpooners — long harpoons that keep the enemy at a distance. |
| **Spark Archer Barracks** | 3 | Hires Spark Archers — arrows with an amber spark that strike from afar. |
| **Infirmary** | 3 | The wounded come here after battles and return to the ranks healed. Every level adds beds and speeds up healing. |
| **University** | 5 | Sciences and troop tiers. |
| **Legion Hall** | 5 | How many fighters a rally march can take; lets you ask your legion for help. |
| **Scout Tower** | 5 | Scouting: look into another castle and learn of a raid in advance. |
| **Workshop** | 5 | Repair and craft: equipment, components and parts; profession crafting. |
| **Shop** | 5 | Trade. |

{% hint style="info" %}
**Gathering opens at Castle level 3.** Before that, your first resources come from the Keeper's Path rewards.
{% endhint %}

## Building and upgrading

1. Enter the castle (**H**) and click a building's island.
2. The card shows the cost, the time and what the next level gives.
3. Press **Build** or **Upgrade**.

A finished construction may complete a **castle quest** — its reward is paid by itself. See [Castle Quests and Speed-ups](castle-quests-and-speed-ups.md).

## Legion help

If you are in a legion and have built the **Legion Hall**, you can ask your legion to help with a construction or a research. Every member who helps takes a little time off. See [Legion Help](../legions/legion-help-and-weekly-goal.md).
