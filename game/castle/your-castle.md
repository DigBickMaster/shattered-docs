# Your Castle

Your castle stands on your shard, on your faction's half of the world. It is where your resources are kept, your army is hired and your hero returns.

Press **H** to enter the castle and again to return to the map.

## How the castle grows

* The **Main Castle** is the heart of your domain. Its level — from **1 to 25** — opens new buildings and sciences.
* Every other building has its own levels. A building can never be higher than the Castle.
* The castle **builds one thing at a time** — any building. While the build queue is busy, wait or use a speed-up.
  At release, a second construction slot will be offered as a separate purchase — see [Subscriptions](../economy/subscriptions.md).
* Higher Castle levels also need the **University** and the **Wall** to keep up. The building card shows what is missing.

## Resources

Everything is built with three resources:

| Resource | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Iron** | Asteroids |
| **Energy** | Asteroids |
| **Crystal** | Asteroids — the rarest of the three |

Resources come home with your **gatherers**. See [Asteroids and Gathering](../army-and-map/asteroids-and-gathering.md).

If a build is missing resources, the card tells you how much — and that the answer is the asteroids.

## Protected stock

The **Warehouse** keeps part of your stock **protected**: a raid cannot steal it. Upgrade the vaults to protect more. There is no storage limit — only the protected part matters in a raid.

## The castle in numbers you see

The castle screen always shows your stock, the queues (construction, research, hiring, healing) and what each building does. You never need to calculate anything yourself.
