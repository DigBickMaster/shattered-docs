# Seals and Resources

Shattered Worlds has a few kinds of wealth, and each has its own job.

| What | Used for | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Seals** | The hero's money: gear in Tala's shop, the market, Healers, Mercenaries and profession services | Goals and daily tasks, won battles in the Circle of War, dungeon chests and bosses, selling items back, profession work |
| **Iron, Energy, Crystal** | Everything in the castle: buildings, research, troops, healing; the legion treasury | Gatherers on asteroids, castle quests, raids |
| **Speed-ups** | Shortening castle timers | Goals, castle quests, dungeons, the legion's weekly goal, the shop |
| **Raw materials** | Profession crafting | Digging at your castle's storehouses (professions) |

## Seals and the castle are separate

**Seals** belong to the Keeper and are spent on the hero side of the game. **Iron, energy and crystal** belong to the castle. One cannot be turned into the other.

## Rewards are known in advance

Goal and daily-task rewards are shown before you take them and are the same for everybody.

{% hint style="info" %}
Items cannot be handed from one player to another. Players trade only what professions make, through the market. This keeps the economy fair.
{% endhint %}
