# The Shop and the Market

## The Shop of Tala the Incorruptible

_"One price for all. No haggling — and no cheating. What you buy is what you get."_

Tala sells **gear** for every class, subclass and level for **Seals**.

* The shop shows items for your hero. The card frame tells at a glance: **green** — better than what you wear, **plain** — the same or worse, **dim** — too early for you.
* Click a card to pin it and compare.
* An **empty slot** on the hero doll opens the shop on the right tab.
* Your **weight limit** is checked when you buy.

### Selling back

Tala buys items back. Up to a certain level she pays the full price; for higher-level gear she pays a part of it. Take worn items off in the hero window before selling.

## The market

The **market** is where players trade — **only profession goods**: grenades, stones, scrolls and socket stones. The shop's own wares are not traded there.

* **Selling:** a hero with a profession puts up a lot with a price in Seals. A lot stays for a limited time, then returns to the bag. A small fee burns on sale.
* **Buying:** a purchase always takes **the cheapest lot** of that kind. You cannot choose the seller, and you cannot buy your own lot.

{% hint style="info" %}
If nobody has put anything up yet, the market is empty — and Tala's own goods are still in the shop.
{% endhint %}
