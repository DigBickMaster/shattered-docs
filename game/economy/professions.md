# Professions

At **level 15** every hero chooses a **profession**. It is chosen **once and for ever** and does not change the hero's combat skills. Professions are how heroes earn Seals from each other — the game does not sell what they make.

## Crafters

They make goods for battle pockets and sockets. Goods are crafted in the **Workshop**, take time, and use **raw materials**.

| Profession | Makes |
| --- | --- |
| **Alchemist** | Metal grenades: **Basic Grenade**, **Stun Grenade**, **Area Grenade** (hits the target and its neighbours) |
| **Gemcutter** | **HP Stone**, **Mana Stone**, **Shield Stone** for pockets; **Socket Stones** — Elemental Attack, Elemental Defense, Resistance |
| **Runescribe** | Scrolls: **Ally Healing**, **Cleansing** (removes negative effects from an ally, except a stun), **Resurrection** |

## Buffers

They cast **buffs** on themselves and one more player. A buff lasts an hour, then has a cooldown.

| Profession | Buffs | Who can receive them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Foreman** | Construction Speed, Research Speed, March Gathering | Your side; free for legion allies |
| **Quartermaster** | Troop Recruitment, Troop Healing, Combat Experience | Your side; free for legion allies |
| **Shaman** | March Speed, Troop Attack, Troop Defense | Any player of your faction |

You can ask a buffer for a buff from the Professions window; they accept in **Incoming**, and the buff is on you at once.

## Services

| Profession | Service |
| --- | --- |
| **Healer** | Heals other heroes' **injuries** at once, for Seals. A Healer cannot heal themselves. |
| **Mercenary** | Joins a **running battle** on the side of the player who hired them, for an agreed price. Can also attack a player who is in the game. |

### Mercenary rules

* A Mercenary is called only into a duel or a group battle that is already going on. The other side answers within a minute.
* The Seals are held until the battle ends. The Mercenary is paid **whatever the outcome**, if they made at least one move; otherwise the Seals go back. A small fee burns.
* A Mercenary gets **no experience** and **no record** from the battle.
* A Mercenary can attack a player who is in the game and not in a battle; that player is then protected from Mercenaries for an hour.

## Raw materials and digging

Crafting needs **raw materials** — iron, crystal or energy dug out by the hero.

* In the Professions window, choose **Digs at the storehouse**: an hour of digging at one of your castle's storehouses gives a handful of raw material, then the hero rests for a while.
* The castle's stock is **not spent** by digging — you need the storehouse built.
* Raw materials are for crafting only and cannot be traded.

## Selling what you make

Profession goods are sold on the **market** — see [The Shop and the Market](the-shop-and-the-market.md).
