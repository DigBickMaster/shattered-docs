# Subscriptions

Separate purchases that save time — never power. Coming at release.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Coming at release.** Subscriptions arrive only after Phase 1 and Phase 2. During both test phases the team hands out many speed-ups to everyone so that testing goes fast. See [Phases](../getting-started/phases.md).
{% endhint %}

**The game sells time, never power.** A subscription gives no stronger troops, gear or skills — it gives you more done in the same time.

| Purchase                    | What it gives                                                                                                  |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Second construction slot**| Your castle builds **two** things at a time instead of one                                                     |
| **Second research slot**    | The University researches **two** sciences at a time instead of one                                            |
| **Both slots**              | A bundle of the second construction slot and the second research slot                                          |
| **AI connection**           | We provide an AI that plays your account 24/7, the way you set it up. See [AI Agents](../community/ai-agents.md) |

* Each is a **separate purchase** with its own price. Prices are in **euros** and will be published before release. AI connection is planned at about **€25 a month**; shorter periods, such as a day, will also be offered.
* Without a slot, the castle builds one thing at a time and the University researches one science at a time — see [Your Castle](../castle/your-castle.md) and [Research](../castle/research.md).

## Where you pay

* **Subscriptions** — in the game, through **Google Play** and the **App Store**.
* **Everything Amber** — only on the **website**, because the game apps cannot use crypto. See [Your Wallet](../amber/your-wallet.md).

Speed-ups stay where they are today: see [Castle Quests and Speed-ups](../castle/castle-quests-and-speed-ups.md).
