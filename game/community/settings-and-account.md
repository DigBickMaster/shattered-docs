# Settings and Account

## Game settings

| Setting | Where | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Language** | The login screen and the settings | Switches the game between **English** and **Russian**. |
| **Less motion** | The login screen and the settings | Turns off pulsing and sliding animations. |

## Your account

* **Password.** Choose a strong one — the meter shows how strong it is. Changing the password ends **every other session** of your account; only the current one stays.
* **E-mail.** For password recovery. Other players never see it. An empty field removes the e-mail.
* **Bio.** Up to **200 characters**, without links. It is shown on your public Keeper page.

## Your name

Your hero's name is 3 to 16 characters: letters, digits, `_` and `-`. It is how others see you in chat, in the Circle of War and on the map.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Never share your password with anyone. The game staff will never ask for it.
{% endhint %}
