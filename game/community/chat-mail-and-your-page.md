# Chat, Mail and Your Page

## Chat

| Channel | Who reads it |
| --- | --- |
| **World** | Everybody — **both factions** |
| **Legion** | Only the members of your legion |
| **Private** | You and one other Keeper |

* **Right-click a line** in the chat to write to that player privately, or type a nick.
* You can **ignore** a player — their messages are hidden from you.
* You can **star** players you want to keep an eye on.

## Mail

Mail brings:

* **Battle reports** — sieges, battles for asteroids, monsters, with coordinates and losses;
* **Scout reports** — the defenders and what can be taken;
* **Alarms** — an army or a scout on its way to you;
* **Letters** — from the game and its characters, often with rewards to claim.

## Your public page

Every Keeper has a **public page**: the hero's level, class and subclass, the worn gear, the Castle level, the record in the Circle of War and a short **bio**. Other players open it from the chat, the Circle of War or the map.

* The bio is up to **200 characters**, without links.
* Only worn items are shown — not your bag and not your pockets.

## Account settings

* **Change the password** — every other session of your account ends.
* **E-mail** — for password recovery later. Other players never see it.
