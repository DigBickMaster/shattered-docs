# Decision Arena

The one-on-one card duel of our world — coming into Shattered Worlds in Phase 2.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Coming in Phase 2.** Decision Arena is not in Shattered Worlds yet. See [Phases](../getting-started/phases.md).
{% endhint %}

**Decision Arena** is a one-on-one card duel by Electrum Decisions. It already exists as its own game: it is published in **Google Play** and the **App Store** and runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS / iPadOS and the web. Its servers come back online at its relaunch.

In Phase 2, Decision Arena opens **inside Shattered Worlds** — a place to play while your castle timers run.

## Decision Arena in short

|                  |                                                |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **Format**       | One on one, turn by turn                        |
| **Cards**        | 99 unique cards: red, blue and green            |
| **Turn**         | 60 seconds                                      |
| **Cards cost**   | Nothing — the cards are free for everyone      |
| **Who you meet** | Keepers of both factions and of every server    |

The cards are game cards. They are not NFTs and cannot be sold.

## What Decision Arena is not

Decision Arena matches **do not decide** sieges, battles for asteroids or wars. It is a game of its own inside the world.

{% hint style="info" %}
Decision Arena is not the [Circle of War](../circle-of-war/the-circle-of-war.md). Heroes fight in the Circle; cards are played in Decision Arena.
{% endhint %}
