# How a Decision Arena Match Works

Castles, armour, three colours of mana and the turn timer.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Coming in Phase 2.** These are the rules of today's Decision Arena. They may be tuned before it opens in Shattered Worlds.
{% endhint %}

## The start

Each player has a **castle** with **30 health** and **10 armour**. Armour works as a shield: damage takes armour away first, then health. Some cards strike past the armour.

## Mana

There are three colours of mana: **red**, **blue** and **green**. Each colour has its own **income** every turn. A card is played with mana of **its own colour**: 33 cards of each colour, 99 in all.

## Cards

Cards can:

* deal damage;
* restore armour or health;
* raise your income — or steal or block the opponent's;
* steal the opponent's mana;
* knock cards out of the opponent's hand.

## The turn

A turn has **60 seconds**. When the deck runs out, it is shuffled and the match goes on.

## The end of the match

A match ends when:

* a castle falls to **0 health** — its owner loses;
* a castle grows to **50 health or more** — its owner wins;
* a player **surrenders**;
* the match ends in a **draw**.
