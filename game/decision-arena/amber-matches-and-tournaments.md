## Free matches

Free matches are open to every Keeper at any level. No Amber is needed, and no Amber is won or lost.

## Amber matches

Amber matches open at **hero level 15**, together with your wallet. See [Your Wallet](../amber/your-wallet.md).

* **Before the match** each player puts in an **Amber entry**: from **10** to **250,000 Amber**.
* **During the match** no Amber moves. The match is pure play.
* **After the match** the entries are split:

| Share   | Goes to                                                                                       |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **90%** | The winner                                                                                    |
| **6%**  | Burned for ever                                                                               |
| **3%**  | [Syra's Treasury](../amber/amber.md#syras-treasury) — Amber staking       |
| **1%**  | The Amber fee — Electrum Decisions                                                             |

* **A draw** returns every entry in full.
* The **1% Amber fee** is taken from every Amber match, in Amber.
